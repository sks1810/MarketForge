Sense · Business Reality Awareness

Stock and demand, this week

Daily sales history is turned into a 7-day demand forecast with a P50 to P90 range. Products whose worst-case demand exceeds stock are flagged before anyone promotes them.

Sample shop: Thrissur Weaves · 56 days of example sales

ModelPrototype forecaster: same-weekday averages over 4 weeks, adjusted for recent trend and planned promotions (a known-future input), with quantile bands. The full build replaces this with a Temporal Fusion Transformer served from a FastAPI service. Status rule: at risk when P90 weekly demand exceeds stock; overstock above 60 days of cover.

Create · CTR-driven ad images

Ad image studio

The real product stays untouched while the background changes with the campaign context. Three variants are rendered and scored for relevance and visual contrast, and the best one is kept.

PipelinePrompt model picks scenes from the context → renderer composes product and background → scorer ranks them. In this prototype the scenes are drawn procedurally and scored from pixels (product/background contrast and colour) plus scene-context match. The full build uses rembg, Stable Diffusion with ControlNet, and CLIP scoring, later a reward model trained on your own clicks.
Deliver · RedQueen scheduling

Smart scheduler

Your latest post sinks one place each time a competitor posts. RedQueen posts with an urgency proportional to how deep it has sunk. Here it races a fixed 10 am / 7 pm calendar over the same competitor activity, with the same number of posts.

u(t) = √(q/s) · r(t) · b

Quiet hours 11 pm to 7 am, at most 4 posts a day. Visibility is measured only while followers are awake.

SimulationCompetitor posts arrive at random, busier in the evening and quiet at night. The full build replaces the simulated rank with a visibility-decay estimate from Instagram Insights, and publishes through a BullMQ or Celery job queue backed by Redis.
